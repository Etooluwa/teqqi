Digital Product / SaaS Platform

Kliques.

A relationship-focused platform designed to help independent service providers manage clients, bookings, payments, and ongoing engagement from one coherent experience.

View Kliques

The Challenge

Client work creates connected demands.

Independent service providers often coordinate relationships, bookings, payment details, preferences, and follow-up across separate tools. That fragmentation can make it harder to maintain a clear view of each client relationship.

The Product Opportunity

Put the relationship at the center.

Kliques brings the essential parts of service delivery into a focused product model, helping providers keep important client context close to the activities that move the relationship forward.

The Solution

A clearer operating space for service providers.

The product experience is organized around the recurring work visible in the Kliques platform: client management, bookings, payments, notes, preferences, milestones, and relationship history.
